# Assets: env/east_asia

All meshes are **procedural and original**, generated by `pipeline/blender/env_eastasia.py` (Blender, bmesh, vertex colours, no textures, no downloads). They reuse the Scandinavian helpers in `pipeline/blender/env_nordic.py` and the shared foliage and ground shaders. Hokkaido and Manchuria also reuse the merged Nordic `env_spruce_a` (as Ezo spruce) and `env_birch_a` (as white birch). There are no third-party or CC assets. Licence: same as the repository.
