# Assets: env/arid

All meshes here are **procedural and original**. They are generated by `pipeline/blender/env_arid.py` using Blender bmesh with vertex colours only: no textures, no downloads. The script reuses the helpers in `pipeline/blender/env_nordic.py` and the shared foliage and ground shaders. The arid biome uses no Nordic models. There are no third-party or CC assets, and the licence is the same as the repository's.
