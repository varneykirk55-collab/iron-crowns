# Assets: env/temperate

All meshes here are **procedural and original**. They are generated by `pipeline/blender/env_temperate.py` using Blender bmesh with vertex colours only: no textures, no downloads. The script uses the shared foliage and ground shaders. There are no third-party or CC assets, and the licence is the same as the repository's.

Each hardwood, the hazel and the far belt comes in three baked seasonal variants:
- summer: the base model;
- `_aut`: autumn reds / golds;
- `_win`: bare branches. Oak keeps a few marcescent leaf clumps.

`temperate.gd` swaps between them by the battle's month (hemisphere-aware). Conifers stay evergreen.
