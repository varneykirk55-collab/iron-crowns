# Assets: env/tropical

All meshes are **procedural and original**, generated by `pipeline/blender/env_tropical.py` (Blender 4.x, bmesh, vertex colours, no textures, no external downloads).
They reuse the Scandinavian helpers (`pipeline/blender/env_nordic.py`: `_stone`, `colour_faces`, `jit`, `finish`) and the shared `env_foliage.gdshader` / `env_ground.gdshader`. No third-party or CC assets. Licence: same as the repository.
