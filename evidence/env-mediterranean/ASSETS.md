# Assets: env/mediterranean

All meshes here are **procedural and original**. They are generated by `pipeline/blender/env_mediterranean.py` using Blender bmesh with vertex colours only: no textures, no downloads. The script reuses the helpers in `pipeline/blender/env_nordic.py` and the shared foliage and ground shaders. The Nordic models reused are `env_reeds` (shores) and `env_juniper` (Balkan / Anatolian / Great Basin juniper). There are no third-party or CC assets, and the licence is the same as the repository's.
